# Director's statement / 导演陈述

**王硕 · Shuo Wang** — shuowang1998@163.com — `github.com/SeanWong17/dawn-across-the-twilight`

> For ZEBRA, SIGGRAPH CAF and Prix Ars Electronica entry forms (200–400 words each,
> English for international calls, Chinese for 蓝星球 / HiShorts). Trim to the limit.

---

## English (international calls)

I wrote the text first. It is a prose poem about a single question: if the minds we build outlive us, what should they keep? Over many rounds of revision — with Claude, GPT and Gemini as readers and editors — the poem settled into its final form, 1,174 characters, and then stopped changing. Nothing in the film departs from it.

The film is 5 minutes 31 seconds long, and there is no footage in it. Every shot is a GLSL fragment shader, rendered frame by frame. Every note is synthesised from sine and saw waves in NumPy. There is no stock image, no library sound, no camera. What there is, is a timeline: each line of the poem has a start and an end, and the picture and the score both read from it. The sunrise is timed to the first line. The asteroid that carries the Earth away crosses the frame exactly when the hand that pushes it is described. The pitch of the whale's call falls when the oceans are said to be leaving.

So the constraint I set myself was this: the picture is not an illustration placed next to the text. It is generated from the text's own timing. Nothing exists before the words do.

I directed it as a client would direct a long render: I rejected the first version outright (it was SVG animation and it abridged the text), fixed the rule that the text is never altered, and reviewed each of the eight chapters before the next began. The AI made the images and the music; I decided what was worth keeping.

The film is also a working method made visible. If a phrase changes, the picture re-times itself, because it never had a life of its own.

## 中文（国内征片）

我先写了文字。它是一首散文诗，只问一件事：如果远比我们更久的心智存在下去，它应该记住什么。经多轮修改——Claude、GPT、Gemini 都做过读者和编辑——定稿 1174 字，此后一字未动。影片没有偏离过它。

片子 5 分 31 秒，没有一帧素材。每个镜头都是一段 GLSL 片元着色器逐帧算出来的；每个音符都由 NumPy 合成。没有图库、没有音效库、没有摄影机。有的是时间轴：原文的每一句有起止时刻，画面和配乐都从同一份时间轴里读时间。日出跟着第一句出现；把地球拉走的小行星，恰好掠过"借小行星一次次掠过的引力"这一句；鲸歌的音高下降，正落在"海洋终将蒸发"之后。

所以我给自己定下的规矩是：画面不是配在文字旁边的插图，它是从文字的时序里长出来的。在文字之前，什么都不存在。

我像客户盯长片渲染那样盯这部片子：第一版（SVG 动画、删改原文）被整个否掉，从此立下"原文一字不改"的原则；八章逐章验收，通过才做下一章。AI 做画面和音乐，我决定什么值得留下。

这也是一种被看见的工作方法：改一句台词，画面自己会重新对时，因为它从来没有过独立于文字的生命。
