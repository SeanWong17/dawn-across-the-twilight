# AI disclosure / AI 参与说明

Required by most festivals now, and worth putting in the entry email before anyone asks.

---

## English

This film was made with substantial AI involvement, in three separable ways. We state them
separately because they are not the same thing, and conflating them misrepresents the work.

**Text — written by the author, revised with AI.** The prose poem was written by 王硕
(Shuo Wang), who holds the copyright. It then went through many rounds of discussion and
revision, in which Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI) and Gemini (Google) acted as readers and
editors: suggesting line breaks, pressure-testing the astronomy, proposing and rejecting
phrasings. The author decided what stayed. The final text is 1,174 characters and it appears
in the film word for word — nothing was cut, added or rewritten for the screen.

**Picture, score, edit — produced by AI under the director's direction.** The 24 shots,
the score and the edit were produced by Claude Opus 5.5, which wrote the GLSL shaders, the
NumPy synthesis code and the Remotion edit from the text and the author's direction. The
author retained direction and approval: the first animated version was rejected outright,
the rule that the text is never altered was set by the author, and each of the eight
chapters was reviewed and approved before the next was begun. The author also chose the
final cuts, the soundtrack balance and the end card.

**No third-party material.** No stock footage, no image library, no sampled music, no
sound-effect library, no camera. Every frame is computed by a shader; every sample of audio
is synthesised.

## 中文

本片有实质性的 AI 参与，分三层，彼此不同，混为一谈会歪曲这项工作：

**文本——作者撰写，AI 参与修订。** 散文诗由作者本人写作（作者为著作权人），随后经多轮讨论与修改，Claude（Anthropic）、GPT（OpenAI）、Gemini（Google）作为读者和编辑参与：提出断句，检验其中的天文学设定，提出并否掉一些措辞。留下什么由作者决定。定稿 1174 字，在影片中一字未改，没有为画面删减、增补或改写。

**影像、配乐、剪辑——在导演指导下由 AI 完成。** 24 个镜头、配乐和剪辑由 Claude Opus 5.5 完成，它依据文本与作者的指示编写 GLSL 着色器、NumPy 合成代码与 Remotion 剪辑。作者保留导演与验收权：第一版动画被整个否掉，"原文一字不改"这条原则由作者确立，八章逐章验收、通过才做下一章；最终镜头取舍、配乐平衡与片尾卡由作者决定。

**不含任何第三方素材。** 没有图库画面、没有素材库音频、没有采样音乐、没有音效库、没有摄影机。每一帧由着色器算出，每一个音频采样由合成生成。
